ABOUT FRIGDEX

A journey of cooling solutions since 1979.

Built on hard work, trusted relationships and a passion for serving customers across Andhra Pradesh.

Our Story

We are Frigdex, and our story is an exciting journey that began in 1979. We breathe sugar air and sip coconut water. Our central operations are based in Tanuku, in the West Godavari region of Andhra Pradesh—a place known as part of the state's rice bowl and for the Andhra Sugars factory.

Over the years, Frigdex has grown into a household name in commercial cooling appliances across Andhra Pradesh. We began as a service organisation and transformed into a manufacturing organisation. Later, we expanded to serve customers through a range of dealerships and distributorships.

Today, Frigdex is a single source for commercial cooling appliances—branded products, customised solutions, and service and repair support. We serve both business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-consumer (B2C) customers.

Our philosophy is simple: higher volumes and lower margins help us reach more people. This belief has helped us extend our reach to every corner of Andhra Pradesh.

Our Visionary

Affectionately known as “Pantulu Gaaru,” Ravi Shankar Gaaru is the visionary behind Frigdex. His journey—from a door-to-door mechanic to a respected name in the commercial cooling appliances industry—is one worth knowing.

Driven by hard work, determination and the ambition to build a financially strong future, he built the business brick by brick. We have never relied on shortcuts. Instead, we have trusted our instincts, built confidence through experience and made bold decisions when they mattered.
